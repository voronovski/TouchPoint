# TouchPoint app icon exploration

The concept sheet in this folder explores six visual directions for the TouchPoint app icon:

1. A relationship path with one emphasized touch point.
2. A calendar with one meaningful date.
3. A continuous relationship loop.
4. A central moment surrounded by people.
5. A speech bubble and calendar combined through overlap.
6. A relationship timeline resolving into completion.

The intended palette follows `docs/design-language.md`: indigo as the product accent, with coral, teal, rose, and amber used sparingly for meaningful moments.

## Second exploration

The second sheet uses bolder, more compact silhouettes intended to survive at small app-icon sizes:

1. An abstract `T` built around a single touch point.
2. An interrupted ripple marking one meaningful moment.
3. Two people or parties connected through one deliberate check-in.
4. A four-part annual cycle with one highlighted segment.
5. A timeline arc with one emphasized date.
6. Interlocking continuity loops joined at a touch point.

## Third exploration

The third sheet moves away from literal people and calendar symbols toward more ownable abstract marks: crossing paths, a four-part relationship compass, a saved moment, a disrupted rhythm, two approaching crescents, and a folded `P` monogram.
